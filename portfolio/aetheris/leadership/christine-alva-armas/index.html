01 / Professional biography

Experience with purpose.

A foundation in human-centered service

Originally from Jamaica and now residing in Edmonton, Alberta, Christine Alva-Armas brings to Aetheris Inc. years of professional experience in compassionate healthcare, end-of-life support, patient management, and human-centered service.

Her professional background has been shaped by the responsibility of supporting individuals and families through complex and often deeply personal circumstances.

This work requires exceptional patience, empathy, emotional intelligence, attentive communication, and the ability to recognize the unique needs of each individual.

These qualities form an important foundation for her leadership role at Aetheris.

Shaping the educational vision

As Chief Vision Officer, Alva-Armas will help guide the long-term educational philosophy of Nexus Education, ensuring that technological advancement remains connected to the needs, dignity, and potential of the students it serves.

Her perspective is especially valuable in the development of educational environments that recognize individual challenges without defining students by those challenges.

She brings an understanding that meaningful support begins with listening, careful observation, and respect for the person behind the information.

Keeping students at the center

Within Aetheris, her responsibilities include shaping the institutional vision, contributing to student-centered experience design, supporting accessibility initiatives, and helping establish a culture of compassion, accountability, and educational opportunity.

Working alongside Chief Executive Officer Valene Jordheim, Alva-Armas will help ensure that the company's commercial development remains aligned with its educational mission.

Her appointment reflects Aetheris's commitment to combining advanced technology with leadership grounded in human understanding.
