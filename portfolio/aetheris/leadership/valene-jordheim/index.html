01 / Professional biography

Experience with purpose.

Media, communications & public engagement

Originally from Saskatchewan, Canada, Valene Jordheim brings to Aetheris Inc. a distinguished professional background shaped by years of experience in broadcasting, media, politics, and public communications.

Throughout her career, Jordheim has worked in environments where credibility, sound judgment, adaptability, and effective communication are essential.

Her professional experience has provided her with a strong understanding of organizational leadership, public engagement, stakeholder relationships, and the importance of translating complex ideas into clear, compelling messages.

Professional recognition

Her accomplishments include two notable professional honors:

  • Jim Pattison Broadcast Group Employee of the Year
  • Jim Pattison Group Personal Achiever Award

These verified achievements reflect her commitment to professional excellence, personal achievement, and organizational contribution.

Leading the enterprise

As Chief Executive Officer of Aetheris Inc., Jordheim brings this experience into the educational technology sector, where she will lead the company's corporate strategy, institutional development, operational direction, commercial expansion, and external relationships.

Her leadership will be central to establishing Aetheris as a credible, sustainable, and forward-looking educational enterprise.

Building confidence and connection

Jordheim's background in media and public engagement provides a valuable foundation for communicating the Nexus Education mission to families, educators, institutions, and strategic partners.

She understands that successful leadership depends not only on organizational performance, but also on building confidence, developing relationships, and creating a shared sense of purpose.

At Aetheris, she is responsible for translating educational innovation into a sustainable institution capable of serving students and families while pursuing long-term corporate growth.
